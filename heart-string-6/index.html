Heart String

Welcome to HeartString!

Show fingers on either hand to play a chord. Thumb plays tonic/base. Index plays second chord. Middle plays third. Ring plays fourth. Pinky plays fifth. 🤙 will play sixth, and 🤟 plays seventh.

Chords can be dictated by individual finger or counting. If you hold up thumb, index, and middle for example, it will still play a third chord.

Tilting the whole hand to the left will change a minor chord to a major one, and vice versa for major chords.

Use the "flip hands" button to swap which hand plays the higher octave. Use the mirror shape button to mirror the mapping of your hand.

Pick a key above, then begin. Enjoy!
